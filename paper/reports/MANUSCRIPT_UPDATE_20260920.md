## Paper argument

1. Standalone accuracy does not answer what a score adds beyond declared statistics.
2. CDU measures paired held-out predictive gain when the detector score is added to that reference.
3. Nine frozen detectors illustrate conditional contributions distinct from standalone performance; spline supplies the main descriptive setting.
4. Matched-metric comparison distinguishes conditioning from merely changing the evaluation metric.
5. Probe, sampling, basis-family and control experiments assess the robustness of this comparative view.
6. Score reconstruction provides a separate diagnostic of statistical predictability, not a mechanism or substitute for CDU.
7. Conclude with conditional evaluation as a complementary axis alongside VUS.
