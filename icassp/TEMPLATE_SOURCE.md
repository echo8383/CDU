The 2027 page currently links an example whose comment says ICASSP-2026. We preserve that comment and use the files linked by the 2027 instructions. The sample example references separate image/BibTeX assets; compile our self-contained `main.tex`, not the archived sample by itself.

Current Paper Kit requirements relevant to this draft: up to four pages of technical content; optional fifth page limited to references, funding acknowledgements, and ethical compliance statement. It specifies two columns, no page numbers, and non-blind authorship. All authors need ORCiD IDs. It lists September 16, 2026 as the paper deadline. Check the official portal for the submission cutoff time; no timezone is assumed here.

The `.bst` file is IEEEbib, not the ICLR natbib style. No ICLR-specific files are needed. The working draft has author placeholders because actual names and affiliations have not been supplied; they must be replaced before submission.
