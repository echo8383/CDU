\begin{abstract}
Sophisticated time-series anomaly detectors do not always outperform simple statistical scorers. What do their scores add beyond those statistics? We introduce conditional detection utility (CDU), the held-out log-loss reduction from adding a detector score to a fixed statistical reference. On 350 univariate series from 23 sources, we evaluate fixed scores from nine detectors with source-held-out probes. Standalone performance and conditional contribution give different rankings even under the same probe and loss. MOMENT-ZS scores are highly reconstructible from the reference ($R^2=0.904$) yet rank ninth by CDU. The leading conditional ranks persist across probe families, sampling seeds, and reference variants. CDU provides a complementary evaluation axis for heterogeneous TSAD scores.
\end{abstract}
